Application purpose
===================

The practical purpose of this tool is mission proposal: help users find
possible mission engagements in a large video game scenario, where they may
not know all the actions available to them. Users should be able to filter
and sort relevant actions.

Standard and hybrid engagement possibilities remain available to the
application's backend, but listing every possible engagement is not itself
the user-facing goal. Keep the interface focused on scenario validation,
filters, and optimizer proposals.

Optimizer proposals are inventory-feasible under the configured model, not
proof that a mission is operationally executable.

The previous implementation plan is archived in archive/ImplementationPlan.txt
for historical context; it does not override this direction.
